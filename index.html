Questions about the worry jar

What is the worry jar?

the worry jar is a free iPhone app for overthinking and anxious thoughts. You type or speak a worry into a glowing jar and set it down. A daily worry window gives worries one gentle time each day, and when you're ready, you breathe out into your phone to release one as a star in your sky.

How does it help with overthinking at night?

At bedtime, drop what's on your mind into the jar. It waits there until your daily worry window, so you don't have to hold it overnight.

What is the worry window?

One gentle time each day to look at what's waiting in the jar. It follows the idea of worry postponement: worries get a set time, so they don't follow you all day.

Is the worry jar free?

Yes. Everything that helps is free. Optional in-app purchases add new glass, skies, sounds and ways to let go.

Is it private?

Your worries stay on your iPhone. There's no account and no analytics, and you can lock the jar with Face ID.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch. You can also add a worry from Siri, Control Center or the Action Button. The app is in English, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Spanish.

Is it a replacement for therapy?

No. It's a small ritual for everyday worries, not a journal, a therapist or a treatment. If worry is getting in the way of your life, please talk to a professional.
